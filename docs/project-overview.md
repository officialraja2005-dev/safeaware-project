# Project Overview

**SafeAware** is a community safety awareness and emergency-support platform
built as a BCA minor project. It combines a safety-concern survey, awareness
campaigns, educational resources, legal-awareness information, an emergency
contact directory, anonymous reporting, and an admin analytics dashboard into
a single application.

## Problem statement

Students and community members often lack a single, low-friction place to:
learn practical safety habits, find the right emergency contact quickly,
voice a safety concern anonymously, and understand general reporting
mechanisms available to them.

## Objective

Build a responsive, database-backed web application that addresses the above
in a calm, non-alarmist way, while being explicit about its limits (it is not
a replacement for emergency services or legal counsel).

## Audience

- **Visitors** — anyone browsing campaigns, resources, and emergency contacts.
- **Registered users** — students/community members who complete the survey,
  submit reports, and manage a profile.
- **Administrators** — manage content and review reports/analytics.
